Ruben Svanerud, brand kit

Logo, colour, type, shapes and templates for Ruben’s films, socials and site. The mark is a camera in disguise: see how.

Download the kit (.zip) Start with the logo
01

The idea: a rune that is a camera

At first glance it’s a rune, a figure with its arms up. Look again and it’s a camera on a tripod.

The strokes are painted, not drawn with a ruler, so the mark feels made by hand like the films. The type stays calm and clean next to it. The edit lives in the words: the R in Ruben and the word cut in rubens.cut are sliced and nudged, like a cut in the timeline.

05

Shapes

Soft leaf and snow-drift silhouettes. Use them big, cropped by the edge and tone on tone: pine on forest, sage on cream. They sit behind content, never on top of photos.

06

Photography

Own work onlyRuben’s own frames. No stock photos and no AI images.
Calm spacePut the mark on a calm part of the photo: sky, snow, water. Never over a face.
