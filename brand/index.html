01

The idea: the frame

Four corners of a viewfinder, drawn by hand. It frames whatever Ruben points it at.

The symbol is a camera frame, and it also reads as a four-leaf clover or a snow crystal. That fits work made on skis, underwater and out in the mountains. The strokes swell at the ends like a brush, so the mark feels made by a person, not a machine. The type is calm and friendly, which lets the films and photos do the talking. A literal cut through the handle rubens.cut nods to the edit.

02

Logo

Clear spaceKeep free space around the logo equal to the height of one corner of the frame.
Minimum sizeLockup 120 px wide on screen (30 mm in print). Symbol 20 px (6 mm).
ColoursCream on forest and on photos, forest on cream, sage and leaf. One colour only.
Don’tStretch, rotate, outline, add shadows or put the logo on busy, light areas of a photo.

05

Shapes

Soft leaf and snow-drift silhouettes. Use them big, cropped by the edge and tone on tone: pine on forest, sage on cream. They sit behind content, never on top of photos.
