Ruben Svanerud: brand kit
=========================

LOGOS (logos/ as SVG, logos/png/ as transparent PNG, 2000 px)
- ruben-svanerud-lockup-*          symbol + name, the default logo
- ruben-svanerud-lockup-stacked-*  for square and portrait formats
- ruben-svanerud-wordmark(-stacked)-*  name only
- rubens-cut-*                     the handle, with the cut through "cut" (-signal = red dot)
- symbol-*                         "the frame": four hand-drawn viewfinder corners
- avatar-*                         profile pictures
Colour variants: forest, cream, ink, leaf. Use cream on forest or photos, and forest on cream, sage or leaf.

COLOURS
Forest  #12392A   main dark, backgrounds, text on light
Pine    #1F6B47   second green, shapes on forest
Leaf    #9FCB6B   bright green, big colour blocks, highlights
Sage    #C6DCC0   soft green, panels
Cream   #F2EBDD   paper, logo on dark and on photos
Ink     #0E1A14   body text on cream
Signal  #E5432D   only the play dot and small calls to action (taken from the red jacket and gondola in his films)

TYPE
Figtree (SIL Open Font License), weights 400–800. Headings 700 with tight tracking (-3.5%), sentence case.
DM Mono (SIL OFL) for small labels and captions only.
Both are free at fonts.google.com.

SHAPES (shapes/)
Four organic leaf/snow-drift shapes. Use them big, cropped by the edge, tone on tone (pine on forest, sage on cream).

APPLICATIONS (applications/)
instagram-avatar, instagram-post, youtube-banner (2560x1440, safe area in the middle), video-end-card (1920x1080),
lower-third and watermark (transparent PNG for editing), business-card-front/back (85x55 mm ratio).
