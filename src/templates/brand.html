01

The idea: the frame

Four corners of a viewfinder, drawn by hand. It frames whatever Ruben points it at.

{{ m.img('ski-gondola', '(min-width: 1025px) 50vw, 100vw') }}
{{ svg('symbol') }}

The symbol is a camera frame, and it also reads as a four-leaf clover or a snow crystal. That fits work made on skis, underwater and out in the mountains. The strokes swell at the ends like a brush, so the mark feels made by a person, not a machine. The type is calm and friendly, which lets the films and photos do the talking. A literal cut through the handle rubens.cut nods to the edit.
