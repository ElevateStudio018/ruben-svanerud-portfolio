Hi, I’m Ruben.

I make films and photographs, mostly on the move: on skis, at ringside, underwater and in the mountains.

I shoot and edit my own work. My short film For the Love of Ski was also my entry to Alpingaraget’s video competition Vinter Edit 2026. In Milan I filmed and edited the first episode of Mr. Popcorn’s YouTube series, which I count as my best work so far.

Alongside the longer films I make short-form video for Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, and I’m open to working with brands and people.
