Filmmaker and photographer in Uppsala and Stockholm.

Ruben Svanerud makes films and photographs. Most of the work happens on the move: on skis, at ringside, underwater and in the mountains.

He shoots and edits his own work. His short film For the Love of Ski was also his entry to Alpingaraget’s video competition Vinter Edit 2026. In Milan he filmed and edited the first episode of Mr. Popcorn’s YouTube series, which he counts as his best work so far.

Alongside the longer films he makes short-form video for Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, and he is open to working with brands and people.
